A few important
details.

This is an independent website design preview for Meridy’s Restaurant & Lounge—not an owner-verified official website.

The site is publicly accessible on GitHub Pages, but asks search engines not to index it. No reservations, payments, orders, or inquiries are collected here. Telephone and direction links go to the published business contact and Maps.

The photography

These are real, licensed photographs used to demonstrate the design. They are not photographs of Meridy’s food, staff, rooms, or service. They must be replaced with approved business photography before an official commercial launch.

Reviews are linked, not reproduced. No invented ratings, testimonials, awards, prices, capacity limits, booking policies, or sourcing claims are used.

Privacy & external services

This site has no forms, advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies, account system, or payment collection. The site itself does not use local storage.

GitHub Pages serves the website, and Google Fonts provides the typefaces; those services receive standard connection information when their resources load. Photographs are served with the website, not requested from Unsplash by visitors. External Maps, Facebook, and Tripadvisor links open their respective services only when followed; their own policies apply.

Font choices are Fraunces and Source Sans 3, with local system-font fallbacks. No embedded map, social feed, WebGL, or Three.js runtime is loaded.

Before an official launch

Confirm the phone, hours, current menu, service offerings, social profile, and operator approval. Supply authentic photography with documented web-use rights. The build has a production gate so that preview disclosures and indexing protection cannot be removed just by toggling a label.
